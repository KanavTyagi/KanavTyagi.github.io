About me

I like the power side of embedded work: the firmware that runs a converter, tuning a control loop until it settles properly, and the hardware underneath it all. My favourite part of a project is getting it from a sketch to something I can put a scope probe on.

At Leonardo DRS I wrote and tuned firmware for power conversion on TI microcontrollers, checked control loops in PSIM before they touched hardware, and worked with CAN bus on UPS systems for naval ships. Now I'm looking for a new-grad role in embedded, firmware, or hardware engineering.

Selected projects

Fall 2026

Wireless Mobile Marine Cargo Crane

A remote-controlled crane running FreeRTOS on a dual-core ESP32. The limit switches fire interrupts that cut the motor PWM straight away, without waiting for a task to notice. A handheld ESP32 remote sends commands over ESP-NOW, and a watchdog catches it if the link drops. I also designed the remote's two-layer PCB.

ESP32FreeRTOSESP-NOWKiCad
Winter 2025

XM23 CPU Emulator

A 16-bit CPU emulator I wrote in about 4,800 lines of C. It runs the full XM23 instruction set, handles nested interrupts and faults, and talks to memory-mapped devices. You can switch the cache simulator between direct-mapped and fully associative, and step through code with the built-in debugger.

CComputer ArchitectureCache Simulation
Fall 2024

Lunar Ice Cave Explorer (LICE)

Software for a rover exploring a simulated lunar ice cave, written in C. It plans its own path, manages its resources, and steers around obstacles, all drawn in the terminal with VT100 graphics.

CPathfindingAutonomy
Winter 2024

Underwater Coral Monitoring Station

An underwater station that logs temperature, pH, and turbidity on a coral reef. I designed the KiCad PCBs for the sensor interfaces and power regulation. It logs data on a microcontroller and sends it out wirelessly from a waterproof 3D-printed housing.
