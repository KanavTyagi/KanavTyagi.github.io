About

p. 01

I like the power side of embedded work: the firmware that runs a converter, tuning a control loop until it settles properly, and the hardware underneath it all. My favourite part of a project is getting it from a sketch to something I can put a scope probe on.

At Leonardo DRS I wrote and tuned firmware for power conversion on TI microcontrollers, checked control loops in PSIM before they touched hardware, and worked with CAN bus on UPS systems for naval ships. Now I'm looking for a new-grad role in embedded, firmware, or hardware engineering.
