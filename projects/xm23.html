XM23 CPU Emulator

Jan 2025 – Apr 2025 · Dalhousie University

An emulator for the XM23, a 16-bit instruction set architecture, written in about 4,800 lines of C. It loads S-record programs and runs them through a fetch-decode-execute loop, with memory-mapped devices, a full interrupt controller and a configurable cache.

What I built

  • An S-record loader, a MAR/MBR bus model and a fetch-decode-execute loop covering the full instruction set, including conditional execution (CEX).
  • Memory-mapped timer, keyboard and screen devices.
  • A priority-based interrupt controller with nested interrupts, tail-chaining, and handling for illegal instructions, illegal addresses, priority faults and double faults.
  • A configurable cache simulator: direct-mapped or fully associative with LRU replacement, write-through or write-back with dirty-bit tracking, and hit-ratio reporting.
  • An interactive debugger with breakpoints and Ctrl+C interrupt.
  • Test cases that validate instruction execution, branching with sign extension and offsets, and device I/O.
