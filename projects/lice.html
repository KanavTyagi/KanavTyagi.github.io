Lunar Ice Cave Explorer (LICE)

Sep 2024 – Dec 2024 · Dalhousie University
LanguageC
DisplayVT100 terminal
Focuspathfinding, obstacle avoidance

Software for an autonomous rover exploring ice caves in a simulated lunar environment, written in C and drawn right in the terminal.

What I built

  • Pathfinding, resource management and obstacle avoidance for the rover.
  • Custom data structures to manage spatial and environmental data efficiently.
  • Terminal graphics using VT100 escape sequences.
  • Testing and iterative refinement for reliability and real-time responsiveness under changing conditions.
